# N07 local research review bundle

Three kinds of evidence remain distinct: original attribution, scientific support and population applicability.

Cards bind exact original bytes and structural annotations. No clinical or human scientific approval has occurred.

Use the .review.json form to record APPROVE, REJECT or REQUEST CHANGES outside this packet, with reviewer identity, competence, authentication, source version and card hash. Leave this immutable bundle unchanged. Conflicting reviews require independent adjudication and owner acceptance. Any card revision invalidates prior review. The software does not activate a support rule.

Descriptors are inactive research interfaces. No runtime source ingestion, telemetry, model, SQL or clinical execution is connected.
