## Data and operation bounds

Use only the two exact retained originals, source-specific notices,471page frozen extraction/3275chunks,33curated units/34spans and40questions. Original document hashes in services/corpus/sources.mjs are local review-computed pins, not independent publisher checksums. Fixed registered files require SHA/size/type and file-instance validation with Linux fd-relative no-follow reads and no hardlink aliases. No network, new acquisition, parser installation, OCR, source execution, embedding or LLM inference.

New catalog records preserve exact text with UTF-16 offsets, physical PDF page separately from printed label, source/version/rights and canonical locator. Subject/context/applicability are retained annotations, not automated scientific judgments. Numeric-looking tokens may be inventoried as unapproved text occurrences; they never become validated values or medical thresholds. No whole-document clinical entailment, population inference or table normalization. Claim assessment reuses unchanged N14v2 canonical source/role/scope binding. Complete quotation ACCEPT means attribution only. Factual/paraphrase/numerical support without independent evidence abstains or rejects. No N15positive support rule is activated.

Review submissions are inert external records with unique IDs, exact source/catalog/case versions, reviewer identity/role/date, scientific axes/reasons and supporting references. Engineering validation reports STRUCTURALLY_VALID_UNAUTHENTICATED only. Actual human independence, qualifications, signatures, adjudication and owner acceptance must be independently established; no software-created field authenticates them. Original pending records remain unchanged. Disagreement is DISPUTED; missing decisions/approval remain PENDING. No clinical/RAG rule admission follows from any submission.

The overall case decision evaluates its proposed answerability/behavior boundary. ACCEPT of an unsupported or partial case can approve its required abstention; it must not falsely mark the entire requested claim entailed. Such records require REJECT/UNCERTAIN on complete-request entailment with reasons. Supported-case ACCEPT requires all declared support axes ACCEPT. This is a structural consistency control, not an automated scientific judgment. Publication deadlines/cancellation are cooperative at phase and bounded-read boundaries; hung filesystem calls can exceed the deadline until control returns. No hard wall-time guarantee for hung I/O is claimed.

Process-local capabilities distinguish evidence-only consumers from review inspection. CLI runs with explicitly identified local project operator authority, not HTTP identity or invented reviewer rights. Input JSON never grants caller authority. No crew/private data or operational application constructor/database exists in this stack. N02, N07–N12, N14/N15 policies and N16 remain unchanged.
