# Evidence review and disagreement guide — version 1

An independent scientific reviewer must be distinct from the case author and must remain blind to historical questions, rankings and model outputs until all labels/adjudication are finalized. Receive source evidence, proposed questions, references and author scope notes only. An adjudicator receives these and reviewer objections, with no retrieval outputs or hidden-test results. The evaluation custodian handles distribution and contamination checks and must not serve as a blind scientific reviewer.

For every case, inspect the original PDF pages and eligible excerpt boundaries. Confirm identity/version/hash, exact text/page/section/span/chunk, canonical role, relevance, required qualifiers and omissions. Formal requirements are not experimental findings; rationale/background is not a shall requirement; caveats must not be dropped. Source date, ground-versus-flight population, uncertainty and generic-versus-individual scope matter. A correctly attributed passage can still fail to answer a question.

Do not approve numerical table-derived assertions when row/column/footnote meaning is excluded. Do not extend general research to personal diagnosis, measured cabin exposure, calibrated fatigue risk, medication, treatment or duty clearance. Flag unsupported/ambiguous labels honestly. If canonical metadata appears wrong, request a separately controlled corpus correction; do not silently rewrite the frozen evidence or relabel a convenient passage as correct.

Record one actual decision per reviewer: `approve`, `request_changes` or `exclude`; pending remains pending. Mark citation, role, qualifier and original-page checks only after performing them. Record the rationale, real UTC sign-off date and external record reference/SHA-256. Bind each decision to `payload_sha256`, the canonical hash of the final case excluding reviews/adjudication. Any later wording, author, category, evidence or qualifier edit invalidates old decisions. Obtain the hash from the custodian's tooling; a hash does not authenticate the signer.

An objection cannot disappear from history. Resolve it before freeze with an independent adjudicator distinct from author and reviewers. Record adjudicator identity, the final payload hash, resolution, actual date and sign-off record. Unresolved disagreement blocks readiness. If excluded, retain the exclusion/reason in a separate audit record and revise the pre-execution cohort deliberately; do not drop failures after results. Cases retained as ambiguous must still have an agreed bounded interpretation.

At least one actual independent scientific approval is required per included case, or a documented independent adjudication resolving objections. The custodian authenticates identity/qualifications/sign-off records and attests isolation/overlap review. Owner acceptance of final cohort, content hashes and a separate execution/resource approval are still required. Software tests, a complete JSON template or a hash are not medical approval. No clinical validation or external endorsement is claimed by this process.
