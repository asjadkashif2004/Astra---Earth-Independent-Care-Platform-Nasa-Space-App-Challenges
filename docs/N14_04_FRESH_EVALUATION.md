- Question authors receive only the approved33curated units and their original-bound source/section/role/caveat context. - Independent appropriately qualified scientific reviewers verify evidence support,required qualifiers,source role/version and minimal citation sets. Medical-scope decisions require appropriate medical review. The author,implementation agent and automated checks do not approve their own cases. Resolve disagreement before freezing; unresolved cases stay ambiguous/unapproved.
